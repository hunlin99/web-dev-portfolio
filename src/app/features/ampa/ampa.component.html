01 / CONTEXT

Project Overview

Connecting cultural identity with a functional digital product.

AMPA is a marketplace concept for the Afro-diaspora, bringing culturally relevant discovery and digital commerce together. The design scope spans brand identity, consumer and vendor journeys, mobile interfaces, and responsive web experiences.

The central design question is how a recognizable brand can support practical workflows for two different user groups, rather than existing only as a visual layer.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

One community. Two sets of needs.

Afro-diaspora consumers need accessible ways to discover culturally relevant products, services, and businesses. Independent vendors need practical tools to reach customers and manage their operations.

How might we create a marketplace that feels culturally authentic while remaining intuitive, scalable, and useful for both consumers and vendors?
